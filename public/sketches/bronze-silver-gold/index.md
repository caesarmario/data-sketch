## Reading the sketch

### Bronze: raw landed data

The bronze layer receives data from source systems with minimal transformation.

### Silver: cleaned and standardized data

The silver layer cleans, standardizes, and deduplicates the landed data.

### Gold: business-ready trusted data

The gold layer turns prepared data into datasets for business use.

## Takeaway

Each layer has a distinct job: land the source data, improve its quality, then shape it for business use.
