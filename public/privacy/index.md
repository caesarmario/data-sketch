# Newsletter privacy

Canonical page: https://datasketch.caesarmar.io/privacy

Updated: 2026-10-21

## Your newsletter subscription

I use the email address you submit to send Data Sketch and manage your subscription. Beehiiv provides the signup form and handles email delivery, subscription preferences, and unsubscribe requests.

## Information beehiiv processes

Beehiiv processes your email address and subscription information. Its service may also process your IP address, browser information, and email opens or link clicks. Its privacy policy explains its data practices and international processing.

## Cookies and website visits

The embedded beehiiv form can use cookies to remember referral information and subscription status. Data Sketch can also use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure website visits. Your email address stays in the signup form; this website does not send form values to its own analytics.

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe using the link in a Data Sketch email. To ask about your subscription or request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email newsletter@caesarmar.io.
